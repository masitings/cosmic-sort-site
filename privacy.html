Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Cosmic Sort is a puzzle game for iOS and Android made by Tech Canvas Studio (Rafi Bagaskara Halilintar), referred to below as "we". This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your game progress is saved on your device.

Data the game collects

DataWhyWho processes it
Gameplay events, such as levels started and finished, moves and boosters usedTo find levels that are too hard or too easy and improve the gameGoogle Firebase Analytics
Crash reports: device model, OS version, app version and the state of the app when it crashedTo find and fix crashesGoogle Firebase Crashlytics
App instance ID and, if you allow it, your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID)To count users, show ads and measure themFirebase and our ad mediation partner (Unity LevelPlay) and the ad networks it works with
Player ID and display name from Game Center or Google Play GamesTo show leaderboards and achievements, only if you sign inApple or Google
Purchase recordsTo deliver and restore in-app purchasesApple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details

Ads and your consent

The game is free and shows ads. On first launch the game explains this and then shows a consent form for personalized ads where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. On iOS, Apple's tracking prompt asks whether apps may use your advertising ID. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalized. You can change your choice any time from Settings › Privacy options in the game, or in your device settings.

Buying "Remove Ads" or the Starter Pack turns off the ads that play between levels. Rewarded ads you choose to watch stay available.

Notifications

If you turn on streak reminders, the game schedules local notifications on your device. No data is sent to us for this.

Children

Cosmic Sort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, who process it for the purposes described here under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase
  • Unity (LevelPlay)
  • Apple and Google for Game Center, Google Play Games and store purchases

Retention and deletion

Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase for the periods set in its service, then deleted. Your progress lives on your device, and uninstalling the game removes it along with the app instance ID. Because the game has no accounts, we cannot look up data by your name or email. If you want data deleted, contact us and we will help with what we can identify, within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us to use any of these rights.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
