Support

Sort the planets so each rocket holds one kind, then watch it launch. Need a hand? Start here.

Contact us: support@techcanvas.studio
Tell us your device model, the app version (Settings, at the bottom) and the level number if it is about a level. We reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap a rocket to lift its top planets, then tap another rocket to drop them in. Planets only land on the same kind or in an empty rocket. Fill a rocket with one kind and it launches. Launch every rocket to clear the level.

I'm stuck on a level

Use Undo, take a Hint, or add an extra rocket with Rocket +1. Restart is always free. Every level can be solved.

My purchase did not arrive, or I changed devices

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack come back this way. Coin packs are used up when spent and cannot be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple or Google Play.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

Open Settings › Privacy options in the game. On iOS you can also change tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Leaderboards are not showing

Sign in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), then open Ranks again.
